ViperX runs on **OKX X Layer mainnet** (chain ID `196`) since 2026-11-16. The vault, the router, the risk guardian and the four TapeOut risk circuits all live on mainnet, because the TapeOut factory and processors exist only there. This page lists every deployed contract, the operations you interact with, and how to check them yourself.

<Note>
  The collateral on mainnet is **demo USDC** (`MockUSDC`) with a public faucet, and prices come from a mock Pyth oracle set to live market prices. No real funds are at risk. You only need a little OKB for gas: a gated trade costs about 0.00002 OKB.
</Note>

## Key on-chain operations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="openPosition: a direct trade from viperx.site/trade">
    `ViperVault.openPosition()` opens a long or short position against your deposited collateral. Before the position opens, the vault calls the circuit guardian, which evaluates all four TapeOut circuits.

    **Who signs it:** Your wallet.

    **What can stop it:** Any failed circuit reverts the trade with `CircuitRiskRejected(diagnosticBits)`. The vault's own checks also apply: a 25x per-market cap, a minimum position size and open-interest limits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="executeAgentIntent: a trade signed by an AI agent">
    `PositionRouter.executeAgentIntent()` takes an EIP-712 intent signed by an agent's key, checks the signature and expiry, then calls `TapeOutCircuitGuardian.validateIntent()`. Only if all four circuits pass does the trade reach the vault.

    **Who signs it:** The agent key signs the intent (market, side, size, collateral, max slippage price, deadline, nonce); anyone can relay it. Collateral is pulled from the agent's own address.

    **Who owns the position:** The agent. The router pays the vault with the agent's collateral and calls `ViperVault.openPositionFor(agent, …)`, which only an authorized router may call, so the position key, the `PositionOpened` event, closing rights and the payout all belong to the agent.

    **What it never allows:** Withdrawals. An intent can only open a position, within the circuit limits and before its deadline. Each nonce works once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="faucet: claim demo collateral">
    `MockUSDC.faucet()` mints 10,000 demo USDC to the caller. The **Faucet** button on [viperx.site/trade](https://www.viperx.site/trade) calls it for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="closePosition: payout goes to the trader">
    `ViperVault.closePosition()` settles a position and pays the collateral plus or minus PnL to the trader who opened it. Only that trader or an authorized `PositionRouter` can close it, and the payout always goes to the trader's address, never to the caller.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="depositLiquidity and withdrawLiquidity: the counterparty pool">
    Liquidity providers deposit USDC for vault shares and redeem their own shares. The pool is the counterparty to every position.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to verify it yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the wiring">
    The vault points at the guardian, and the guardian points at the processor for each check (`0` leverage, `1` slippage, `2` drawdown, `3` cooldown):

    ```bash
    cast call 0x0e36c7fE3B20b1c33B1691E7041A80ad50e77Bdc "circuitGuardian()(address)" --rpc-url https://rpc.xlayer.tech
    cast call 0x54FaE0b57646365c6e4631E06dFe3d314BA0206A "circuitFor(uint8)(address,uint256,uint32)" 0 --rpc-url https://rpc.xlayer.tech
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Query a circuit">
    `processor.eval` is a read call. Leverage is encoded as leverage × 100, little-endian, so 20x is `0xd007` and 25x is `0xc409`:

    ```bash
    cast call 0xb57b56034e2dc25161e2512CfC40b24EA3bee1Fb "eval(uint256,bytes)(bytes)" 1 0xd007 --rpc-url https://rpc.xlayer.tech   # 0x01, pass
    cast call 0xb57b56034e2dc25161e2512CfC40b24EA3bee1Fb "eval(uint256,bytes)(bytes)" 1 0xc409 --rpc-url https://rpc.xlayer.tech   # 0x00, fail
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trip a circuit from the app">
    On [viperx.site/trade](https://www.viperx.site/trade), connect an OKX Wallet on X Layer, click **Faucet**, open a 5x long, then try 25x. The second order is refused by the leverage circuit and no funds move. The **Circuits** button in the top bar lists every contract above with explorer links.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Registry operations

On X Layer and Base Sepolia the registry is an EVM contract with `registerAgent`, `changeStatus` (Active, Paused or Retired) and `updateMetadata`; only the agent's owner can call the last two. The Solana Devnet program uses the operations below.


<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="register_agent: write your agent identity">
    Creates a permanent on-chain record with your agent ID (up to 32 bytes, unique per wallet), display name (up to 64 bytes), strategy URI (up to 200 bytes) and vault address. Signed by your wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="record_trade: record a fill">
    Writes a fill record. It counts toward your verified total only if the indexer matches it against on-chain position state: size within ±2% of the settled delta, a round trip of at least 10 seconds, and at least $5 in size.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="delegate_authority: grant execution rights">
    Grants the ViperX runtime the right to submit trades and trigger the circuit breaker for your agent. It never includes withdrawal rights, token approvals or the ability to move principal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="authority_pause(): emergency halt">
    Stops all trade execution for your agent at once. The runtime can call it automatically, or you can from your dashboard; only your wallet can unpause.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
