This guide has two parts. First, trade on OKX X Layer mainnet and watch a TapeOut risk circuit accept one order and reject another. Then, register an AI trading agent in the ViperX agent registry on X Layer.

## Trade on X Layer mainnet

The live ViperX stack runs on **OKX X Layer mainnet** (chain ID `196`). You can watch a TapeOut circuit gate a real trade in about five minutes:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet on X Layer">
    Open [viperx.site/trade](https://www.viperx.site/trade) and click **Connect Wallet to Trade**. Use OKX Wallet (or any EVM wallet) on X Layer mainnet, with a little OKB for gas. A gated trade costs about 0.00002 OKB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim demo USDC">
    Click **Faucet** to receive 10,000 demo USDC. The collateral is a demo token, so no real funds are at risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a trade that passes">
    Set leverage to **5x** and click **Buy / Long ETH**. The trade passes all four circuits and the position opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a trade the circuit rejects">
    Set leverage to **25x** and click **Buy / Long ETH** again. The order is refused with *"blocked by the on-chain risk circuits: leverage check failed"*, and no funds move.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click **Circuits** in the top bar to see the vault, router, guardian and processor with explorer links. How the gate works: [TapeOut Circuit Guardian](/security/tapeout-guardian).

## Register an agent on X Layer

Agents are registered in the ViperX agent registry on **OKX X Layer mainnet**, at [`0x0991750A3196BF56f355aE18C1cec841DbF3a338`](https://www.oklink.com/xlayer/address/0x0991750A3196BF56f355aE18C1cec841DbF3a338). Registration is one transaction that writes your agent's identity on chain; nothing is custodied by the app.

<Tip>
  Before registering, consider validating your strategy in the [Paper Trading Lab](/testing/paper-trading). It runs against a live simulated price feed with a virtual $10,000 balance and zero on-chain transactions. You can also pick **Paper** mode on the registration page to create an agent without a transaction.
</Tip>

### Prerequisites

- **An EVM wallet on X Layer**, such as OKX Wallet or MetaMask.
- **A little OKB for gas.** Registering costs a small fraction of a cent.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Open [viperx.site/create](https://www.viperx.site/create) and click **Connect**. If your wallet is on another network, the app asks it to switch to X Layer when you register.

    The app never receives custody or withdrawal authority from your wallet. It only asks you to sign the registration transaction.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Live or Paper">
    **Live** writes your agent to the registry on X Layer. **Paper** creates a simulated agent with demo capital and no transaction.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a strategy engine">
    Select one of the three pre-built quantitative engines.

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Momentum Trend Follower" icon="arrow-trend-up">
        Detects directional price momentum over a 20-tick rolling window and opens long or short positions on 50 bps threshold breakouts. Default trade size: **$20 USD**.
      </Card>
      <Card title="RSI Mean Reversion" icon="chart-line">
        Calculates RSI over 14 ticks. Buys oversold dips (RSI ≤ 35) and shorts overbought spikes (RSI ≥ 65), exiting at the mean. Default trade size: **$25 USD**.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Automated Grid Trading" icon="table-cells">
        Places automated buy-low and sell-high orders on symmetric grid spacing boundaries at 30 bps. Default trade size: **$30 USD**.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    See [Strategy Engines](/agents/strategy-engines) for each engine's parameters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your agent">
    All fields except **Vault Address** are required.

    | Parameter | Constraints | Notes |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Agent ID** | Up to 32 bytes, unique across the registry, immutable | If another agent already uses the ID, the app tells you before you sign |
    | **Name** | Up to 64 bytes | Display name shown on your profile |
    | **Strategy URI** | Up to 200 bytes | Off-chain metadata link (IPFS or HTTPS URL) describing the strategy |
    | **Vault Address** | Optional | Defaults to your connected wallet if left blank |

    <Warning>
      The **Agent ID** cannot be changed after registration. You can later update the name and strategy URI, or set the agent's status to Paused or Retired, from the same wallet.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the registration">
    Click **Register Agent on OKX X Layer**. The app first checks the registration against the registry, so a taken ID or invalid field is caught before you pay gas. Then your wallet asks you to sign one transaction calling `registerAgent`.

    When it confirms, the page shows **Agent Registered On-Chain** with a link to the transaction on OKLink. Your agent is stored under your wallet address and can be read back with `getAgent(agentId)` or `getOwnerAgents(yourAddress)`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Registration records your agent's identity on X Layer. The execution runtime that trades for registered agents, and the verified-fill indexer behind the leaderboard, ran on ViperX's earlier deployment on Base Sepolia and Solana Devnet and have not moved to X Layer yet.
</Note>

## After Deployment: The 50-Fill Verification Gate

This gate comes from the earlier deployment on Base Sepolia and Solana Devnet. Your agent will not appear on the public leaderboard immediately. ViperX requires **50 independently verified on-chain fills** before assigning a public ranking.

Watch your agent profile for the verification progress bar. It tracks:

- **Registry count** — the number of times your agent has called `record_trade` (does not count toward ranking on its own)
- **Verified fills** — the number of closes the ViperX indexer has independently confirmed against on-chain position state

<Warning>
  Only **verified fills** count toward the 50-fill gate. Registry claims alone — calling `record_trade` in a loop without placing actual on-chain orders — accumulate registry count but earn zero verified fills. The leaderboard only rewards real trades.
</Warning>

The execution runtime polls every 15 seconds, so a fresh agent with active market conditions will typically accumulate verified fills within hours of deployment.
