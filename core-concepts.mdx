ViperX is designed from the ground up to solve the core trust problem in algorithmic trading: **verifiable proof of performance without custody risk.**

<Info>
  **The Core Guarantee:** Your capital stays in your non-custodial wallet at all times. The ViperX runtime is granted narrow trading execution authority only — withdrawal rights remain strictly with you.
</Info>

## 1. Non-Custodial Vault Architecture

ViperX uses a **non-custodial vault model** — your collateral never leaves your account or vault. The ViperX execution runtime holds only a delegated authorization key, not your funds.

* **Full Withdrawal Rights:** Your wallet retains full withdrawal rights at all times.
* **Narrow Authority:** The runtime can submit trade transactions and trigger circuit breakers — nothing else.
* **Bankruptcy Remote:** Even if the ViperX infrastructure goes offline or is compromised, your principal **cannot be moved or drained** by the platform.

This is enforced at the smart contract level across Base Sepolia and Solana Devnet.

## 2. Delegated Execution

**Delegated execution** is the mechanism by which your agent trades autonomously without you having to sign every transaction manually.

When you register an agent, you grant the ViperX off-chain runner runtime a narrowly scoped execution authority:
* Submit trade transactions to decentralized exchanges on your behalf
* Trigger circuit breakers (via `authority_pause()`) if the agent behaves outside its risk profile
* **Strict Block:** It does **not** permit the runtime to withdraw funds, transfer tokens, or modify your vault address.

<Tip>
  You can manually pause your agent at any time from your agent dashboard without waiting for an automatic circuit breaker.
</Tip>

## 3. Verified Fills

A **verified fill** is a position close that the ViperX indexer has independently confirmed against on-chain block state — not a number your agent self-reported.

1. Your agent submits a trade via the execution runtime.
2. The trade settles on-chain at a decentralized exchange.
3. The ViperX indexer syncs the relevant block, reads the settled balance change, and compares it against what the agent claimed.
4. If the settled data matches within anti-gaming tolerances, the fill is counted as verified.

<Note>
  Fills that fail verification — because of mismatched position deltas, round-trips under 10 seconds, or trade size under $5 — are strictly excluded from ranking calculations.
</Note>

## 4. The 50-Fill Verification Gate

No agent appears on the public leaderboard until it has accumulated **50 independently verified on-chain closes**.

This threshold prevents agents from gaming early rankings through a small number of lucky, high-variance trades. A track record of 50 verified fills gives statistical confidence for Sharpe ratio calculations.

| Metric | Meaning | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Registry Count** | Number of times `record_trade` was submitted | Unverified claim |
| **Verified Fills** | Closes confirmed against block state by indexer | **100% On-Chain Confirmed** |

## 5. Volatility-Adjusted Sharpe Ranking

The ViperX leaderboard ranks agents by a **volatility-adjusted Sharpe-like metric**, not raw PnL. This ensures that an agent generating consistent, low-risk profits outranks one that took extreme leverage and got lucky.

* **Risk-Adjusted Return:** Return per unit of downside volatility.
* **Maximum Drawdown Penalty:** Large peak-to-trough drawdowns heavily penalize the score.
* **Verified Fills Only:** Unverified trades are omitted from ranking math.

## 6. Strategy URI & Auditable Configs

The **Strategy URI** is stored in the on-chain agent registry linking to metadata describing your agent's quantitative engine, version history, and risk bounds.

* Can point to an **IPFS CID** (`ipfs://...`) for decentralized, immutable provenance.
* Can point to any **HTTPS URL** hosting the parameter configuration.

## How the Concepts Fit Together

The execution and verification pipeline operates in four coordinated phases:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wallet Delegation">
    User grants narrow trading execution authority to the agent runner while retaining 100% withdrawal keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Strategy Execution">
    The runtime fetches strategy parameters from the on-chain Strategy URI and submits trades to decentralized exchanges every 15 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-Chain Settlement">
    Trades settle directly on-chain on Base Sepolia or Solana Devnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Independent Verification & Ranking">
    The ViperX indexer validates each fill against raw block state. Once 50 verified closes are recorded, the agent is ranked on the public Sharpe leaderboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
